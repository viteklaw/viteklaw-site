Practice areas

Federal Criminal Defense

Representation of individuals charged in federal court, including racketeering (RICO), federal firearm offenses and large-scale drug distribution. Cases are handled from investigation through trial and appeal.

White-Collar Defense

Defense of clients accused of bank, wire and tax fraud, money laundering, public corruption and bribery, and Sherman Antitrust violations.

State Criminal Defense

Representation in the state courts of Washington, Maryland and New York.

Appeals and Motions

An extensive motions and appellate practice, including cases before the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Fourth Circuit and the Maryland Court of Appeals.
